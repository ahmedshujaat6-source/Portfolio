Northlane Rebrand & Launch

A full brand refresh and launch plan for a growing studio.

Northlane had grown quickly but its brand and messaging hadn't kept pace. I led a full strategy refresh — positioning, voice, visual direction and a phased launch plan — working closely with their internal team to roll it out without disrupting day-to-day operations.

Highlights

  • Grew qualified inbound inquiries by 3x within two quarters
  • Introduced a brand guide adopted across all client-facing materials
  • Trained the internal team to maintain the new voice and visual system
